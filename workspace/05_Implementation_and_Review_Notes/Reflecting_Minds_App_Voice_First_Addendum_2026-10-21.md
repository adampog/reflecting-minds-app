# Addendum: voice-first conversation option (proposal, not a decision)

Status: product-direction note for a demonstrator, 21 October 2026. It does not change the original specification and does not approve any production platform, speech provider, framework or backend.

## Direction relayed from Alan (via Dot, 17:05Z)
A third option where a person talks and listens through the same workflows A, B and C and the navigation controls, without typing or needing to read a screen. Spoken dialogue carries the workflow, choices and outcomes, with natural pause, repeat, skip, stop and clarify.

## What the demonstrator shows
- A scripted, simulated User/AI conversation following spec §2 Flow A (seven stages), Flow B (worksheet from Discern) and Flow C (return and learn), then navigation (repeat, privacy, report, stop).
- Two preset synthetic voices (local Kokoro). Comic-style speech bubbles and a state line accompany the speech; they never replace it.
- Label on every frame: "Example conversation workflow". All data fictional; all AI turns simulated.

## How the spec's rules carry over to voice (proposed)
| Spec rule | Voice equivalent shown |
|---|---|
| FR03 explain storage and AI before free text | Spoken before the first answer |
| FR04 pause, skip, stop | "Pause" / "Continue" exchange; skip and stop offered aloud |
| FR05–06 own words, mixed/uncertain, no ranking | Examples read as invitations; "no difference is fine"; "no ranking" |
| FR07 interpretation is not fact | AI says the quiet friend is "your interpretation, not a fact" |
| FR08 optional action, no acting for user | "Leave" offered; "I won't contact anyone for you" |
| FR09 plan separate from outcome | Original plan read back unchanged; outcome added as a later review |
| FR10–11 consent per request, contestable AI | AI names the exact three answers it would use; suggestion rejected, no penalty |
| FR12 explicit save | Saving only on "save it"; "not a backup" |
| FR16 exit, privacy, report | Spoken privacy answer, report read back first, "stop" ends at once |

## Open questions a real voice product would add (not decided)
Speech recognition errors and confirmation of what was heard; where audio is processed and kept; whether voice recordings are ever stored; privacy of speaking aloud near others; accessibility for people who cannot speak or hear; language and accent coverage; barge-in and turn-taking. Real-time or full-duplex speech is not shown and not proposed here.
