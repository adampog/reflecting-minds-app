# Addendum: social-media entry walkthrough (fourth video, proposal only)

Status: demonstrator brief, 21 October 2026. Not a product decision; the original specification is unchanged.

## Sources and provenance
- Original app spec v0.1 (unchanged), SHA256 a07e664f6e81dce41ca28d0839cbbc857ecc9fff625bda9ee85a3178bb6ea0ff: §1 J4 (tailor initial presentation; short social content leading to relevant app experiences; hunting and ancient navigation as possible examples); §2 Flow A entry from "a reel, message or QR code"; FR01 (deep links identify content, never a person or inferred ideology); FR02 (person chooses presentation; no assigned identity); §5 (packs lead into the same core cycle; do not present generic cultural experiences); FR13/FR16 (export and sharing through the phone's own user-controlled flow); §12 (separate evaluation).
- "Minds and Understanding" final response, section 5 ("The consolidated direction for Reflecting Minds"): The exact final-response text was not available during preparation; the demonstrator follows the source-grounded specification and the approved fourth-video brief, without quoting that response.

## What the video shows
Two fictional reels with different situations (a tracker at dawn; a navigator disagreeing with her crew) each open a matching authored entrance through a simulated content-only link, then the same practice (notice-v1), lived motivation, optional discernment, optional response (including no action and bounded cooperation), optional user-chosen sharing (default none, preview first, nothing sent), and a later self-initiated return.

## Assumptions (demonstrator only)
| ID | Assumption |
|---|---|
| SA01 | Entrances are situational stories chosen by the viewer, not cultural or demographic categories. |
| SA02 | Links use the reserved domain reflectingminds.example and carry only pack, exercise and locale identifiers. |
| SA03 | The social platform, reels, in-app browser, sharing and saving are drawn as simulated wireframes; no real platform, API, account or post. |
| SA04 | Silent captioned video (speech optional per brief); wireframes reuse the visual language of the earlier mockups. |
| SA05 | Practice text is the same placeholder as earlier mockups and still needs Jake's review. |

## Out of scope
A social network, feeds, comments, groups, follower graphs, analytics, real deep-link handling in social in-app browsers, efficacy or behaviour-change claims.
