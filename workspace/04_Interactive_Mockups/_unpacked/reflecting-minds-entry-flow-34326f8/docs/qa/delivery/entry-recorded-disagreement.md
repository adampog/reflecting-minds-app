# Entry-flow demonstrator: recorded disagreement

**The question.** Should the fictional scene page, which comes just after the privacy explanation, show the door's full-colour plate?

- **Builder's view.** Keep it. The plate is the scenario itself, so it is content, not decoration.
- **Reviewer's view.** Remove it. The scene text already carries the whole scenario, and a bright plate straight after "quiet from here on" breaks the "bright at the door, quiet in the room" boundary.
- **Outcome.** The reviewer's view was adopted, and the scene page is text only. The chosen art direction (A, layered paper) is unchanged at the entry. This is recorded rather than erased.

**Also recorded, not resolved:**
- Art direction A was chosen by Alan against the Art Department's and the builder's lean towards B. The reviewer was asked to judge A on its own terms, and did not soften it towards B.
- The well plate narrowly misses one of the Art Department's equal-weight metrics. The reviewer saw no material outlier at actual thumbnail size, and no further art round was made.
- Whether the displaced historical settings suit someone in live difficulty is untested. A contemporary comparison plate is provided for that review only.
