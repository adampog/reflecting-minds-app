The tap-through viewer and 157.48-second silent captioned video are accepted as a fictional demonstrator of the entry flow. Both entry routes, choice of involvement, privacy explanation, quiet scene and first practice are represented. Later stages retain the quieter treatment. This does not establish production readiness or participant safety.

**Does the entry feel safe to step into, or like being sold something?** More inviting than sales-like. Equal-sized choices, optional language, a neutral route and the absence of sign-up, rewards or benefit promises make it inexpensive to begin or leave. The layered-paper threshold feels deliberately made. That is a reviewer's impression, not evidence that people in difficulty will feel safe.
