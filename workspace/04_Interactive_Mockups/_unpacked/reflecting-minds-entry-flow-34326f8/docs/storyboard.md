 1. Entry · Arrival: warm, two equal ways to look through the doors
 2. List route · All nine doors, plates and text; order shuffled per visit, fixed for this video
 3. List route · Every door is one tap; nothing is ranked or featured
 4. Reel route · Swipe or use buttons; nothing moves on its own
 5. Reel route · Same doors, same text, a list one tap away
 6. Entry · Surprise me picks only after you ask, and you can still choose
 7. Entry · Just curious: not a situation, a shorter path
 8. Entry · Divided group: one person reflecting about a group
 9. Entry · The riverbank: type, language, duration, labelled fictional
10. Entry · How involved to be: fictional, personal or neutral
11. Privacy · Quiet from here on: explained before any typing
12. Scene · A pre-written branch you choose; no AI
13. Notice · The first stage: text practice, optional timer, skip or report
14. Stage menu · Any stage, any order; no progress or completion marks
15. Later stages · Reused from v0.2, same quiet treatment
16. Later stages · Nothing celebrates finishing; leaving is always valid
17. Entry link · Opens the matching door; the link names content only
18. Text only · Every picture is dropped; the text stands on its own
19. Personal route · Privacy first, then your own words
20. Fallback · A retired or unknown link opens a safe page
21. Review only · Displaced or contemporary setting, side by side; untested
22. Reduced motion · Same doors and routes, no motion
23. Exit · Always one tap; leaving is a valid choice

Assertions made during the walk: every swipe changes the card; the scene has no plate; Text only leaves no picture on the door or privacy pages; the reel offers list, Surprise me and neutral.

Known variance: *Surprise me* picks at random, so the door it shows may differ between the dry run and the render.
