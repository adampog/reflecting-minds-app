The model

Seven flexible stages. The sequence is the product, but the user is never held to it.

  1. EntryArrive through one of roughly nine doors — a story, a link, a situation. Doors describe content; they never label people.
  2. NoticeAn optional attention practice. Skippable without penalty.
  3. MotivationDescribe what is actually moving you, in your own words.
  4. CompareOptionally hold a second motivation alongside the first. No ranking. The first answer is preserved.
  5. DiscernAccounts, claims, sources, unknowns, absent perspectives, costs of delay. Interpretations of other people's motives stay labelled as interpretations.
  6. RespondChoose an action, an inquiry, a pause, or an exit. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  7. ReviewReturn later and record what actually happened. The original plan is kept separate from revisions.

At every stage the user may move back, skip optional work, pause, disagree, decline to act, or leave. Mixed, absent, uncertain and "no difference" are valid answers. A neutral route stays usable throughout, and no branch treats refusal as bad faith.

Three core flows

A

Try an experience

Enter, choose a situation, try or skip the attention exercise, describe a motivation, weigh evidence and unknowns, choose a response, then review and optionally save.

B

Examine a disagreement

Open the optional Discern worksheet for accounts, claims and sources, unknowns, affected or absent perspectives, costs of delay and possible responses.

C

Return and learn

Reopen a saved session on the same device, record whether the response happened and what followed. The original record survives alongside revisions.

Privacy posture

Unsaved drafts live in memory only — not in localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, caches, URLs or telemetry. Durable saving requires a deliberate, plainly-named Save on this device action, written transactionally so a failed write leaves the draft intact and says so.

Export is field-selectable with a preview in both text and JSON; deletion is scoped and confirmed, covering linked outcomes and revisions across every journal store. Reflection text is never transmitted — not to servers, not to analytics, not to AI.

The plan is careful about one thing worth repeating: for a hosted app, "nothing leaves the device" must not be claimed merely because journal text stays local. Hosting and CDN logs still see IP addresses, paths and referrers.

Excluded from beta

  • live AI
  • generated summaries
  • classifications
  • voice AI
  • groups
  • shared journals
  • public sharing
  • user invitations
  • research enrollment
  • ratings
  • satisfaction surveys
  • learning prompts

No claims may be made about proven improvements in Global Attentiveness, empathy, agreement, wise action or conflict resolution.

Accountable owners

Four functions must have named people before release. None is assigned. This is the blocking item for Phase 0.

Productunassigned

Requirements, open choices, beta invitation policy, supported devices, launch approval.

Engineeringunassigned

Storage and network boundaries, accessibility implementation, tests, migration and update reliability, rollback.

Contentunassigned

Authored scenarios and practices, independent review, locale assets, corrections, content retirement.

Privacy & operationsunassigned

Hosting and log review, approved telemetry and report handling, retention, incident response, deletion scope.
